Ethics, Integrity and Aptitude v1.0

Prateek Garg · A topic-wise reference on ethics in public life and governance · November 2026 · 188 pages, 64 diagrams

A single reference on ethics in public life and governance: concepts, moral thinkers from India and the world, frameworks, real-world examples and answer-writing tools, with revision tables and summaries throughout. Written for anyone studying ethics, public administration or political philosophy, in India or abroad; it was researched during preparation for India’s Civil Services examination, and follows the structure of that exam’s ethics paper. It includes a chapter on 25 contemporary ethical issues, from euthanasia and surrogacy to AI, refugees and weapons of mass destruction, each with an answer frame, and 64 diagrams that map the ideas at a glance.
